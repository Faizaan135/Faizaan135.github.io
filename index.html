A disciplined design process

  1. DefineProblem, stakeholders, use cases and measurable requirements.
  2. ArchitectTrade-offs, risks, interfaces and design rationale.
  3. ImplementControlled firmware, hardware, RTL and revisions.
  4. VerifyTest procedures, measured results, iteration and reporting.
